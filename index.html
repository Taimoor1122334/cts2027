About CTS

The most innovative conference for the community of faith

The Church & Technology Summit, held at the Experia Summit, equips churches to lead with excellence and vision in an evolving digital world. Exploring the intersection of ministry, leadership, and technology, the summit helps churches leverage tools that enhance worship, operations, communication, and discipleship while preserving spiritual integrity.

Tracks

Insightful tracks. Transformative experiences.

CTS offers engaging, insight-driven tracks designed to equip church leaders, creatives, and technology professionals with practical tools and innovative strategies. Covering topics from emerging technologies and digital innovation to leadership, media, and church operations, each track delivers actionable insights to help churches navigate an evolving digital landscape with confidence and excellence.

CTS at Experia

The Church & Technology Summit (CTS) is part of the larger Experia Summit 2027, alongside the AI Summit and Entrepreneur Summit. Registration to CTS grants you access to all three, creating one unified experience where innovation, entrepreneurship, and ministry intersect.
