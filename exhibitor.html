About CTS

Exhibitors at CTS

The Church & Technology Summit (CTS) is more than a gathering—it's a hub of ideas, innovation, and opportunity. Exhibiting at CTS 2026 positions your brand in front of pastors, ministry leaders, technologists, and creatives actively seeking solutions to grow, scale, and transform their communities.

Whether you serve the faith community, small businesses, or the tech space, CTS offers direct access to decision-makers and real-world application across industries. It's more than a booth—it's an ecosystem where you build partnerships, generate leads, and position your brand for impact.
